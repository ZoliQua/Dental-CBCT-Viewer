# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

This project exists so that dentists, developers and researchers can view CBCT
scans and plan implants together. Everyone who takes part, whether by opening an
issue, sending a pull request or simply asking a question, deserves a welcoming
and harassment free experience. That holds regardless of age, body, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, gender, level of experience, nationality,
appearance, race, religion or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Assume good intent and give people the benefit of the doubt.
- Welcome newcomers and help them find their footing.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Keep the discussion about the work, not the person.
- Remember that this is clinical software, so accuracy matters and an honest
  correction is a courtesy, never an attack.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, demeaning remarks or personal attacks.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Any behaviour a reasonable person would read as hostile or unprofessional.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, including issues, pull requests, discussions
and any channel that represents the project, and also when someone is speaking
for the project in public.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these rules, please contact the
maintainer privately, [@ZoliQua](https://github.com/ZoliQua). Reports are handled
discreetly and in good faith.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests
that break this code, and may warn or ban anyone who will not stop. Decisions are
made as fairly as the situation allows.

This code is offered in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, written in plain
language for this project.
